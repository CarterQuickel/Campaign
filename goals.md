Things to be completed as of 1.0, before submission for the competition:

1. The AI should be able to pick from a random list of pre-made stories and give the players items related to that story.
    - Misc items can also be given, which are not story-specific and can be obtained in any story mode.
    - Gold should be aquirable and spendable, however that might be. For now, gold has no purpose.
    - Your weight should directly benefit or hinder your playthrough. You can equip new tools, potions, and weapons, but only outside of combat. If you have a higher weight, you should get disadvantage. This is not accurate to official DND, but it still works in Charma.

2. Combat should consist of a pre-made script for the AI to follow, where they will be initiating in combat that is not AI-driven. This is to ensure that you can't cheat combat.
    - When in combat, on your turn, a panel will appear with a question and 4 options, using Quizbank questions.
        a. If right, roll.
        b. If wrong, your turn is skipped.
    - On an enemy's trun, they will attack as well. In future updates, streaks will benefit combat.

3. Once a combat scenario is completed, players will gain mastrey and a small amount of XP. Getting high mastery grants more XP.
    - Each course unit has its own mastery path, where 5%, 15%, etc. all grant exponentially more XP upon reaching.
    - There will be a special reward for getting 100% on all units in a course.

Future updates that can be added after 1.0:

1. A streak system will encourage the player to get more consecutive questions right. With a larger streak, your modifier goes up, and therefore, you do more damage. However, the bigger your streak, the harder the detriment when you get a question wrong. If you win in a combat scenario with a high streak, you'll get more XP and slightly more mastery.

2. Notifications would be used on an app version, if that is persued. It would give a basic message to encourage players to hop back on, continuing their streaks.
