[Part 7] - The Mountain of Ash

[The Ascent Begins]

The mountain rises beyond the ruined kingdom, its upper reaches hidden by ash and smoke.

Few scouts have returned from its higher paths. Those who have describe enormous webs, strange creatures, and a sound that can be heard even through solid stone.

The hero begins the ascent at the mountain's base, entering a cavern that winds through the rock.

Inside, the hero encounters the Withstanders: monstrous yokai that carry mystical gongs.

These creatures were once associated with the king's research program. Their gongs produce an unusual frequency that interferes with Alternate X's enhanced hearing and disrupts its ability to coordinate its movements.

The Withstanders were originally intended to help keep Alternate X under control in the event of another rebellion.

But after the kingdom fell, the Withstanders were abandoned in the mountain passages. They now guard the old routes, attacking intruders who approach their territory.

The hero can learn something important from them: the king had discovered a way to exploit Alternate X's enhanced senses, but he never found a reliable method to control the supersoldier permanently.

This is another sign that the king's experiments were built on incomplete understanding.

The deeper the hero travels, the more frequently the walls are covered in thick spider webs.

Something enormous lives ahead.

[Supaida, the Ashen Oni]

The hero eventually reaches a vast cavern covered in webs and ash.

At first, the chamber appears empty.

Then the darkness begins to glow.

A massive demonic spider descends from the ceiling. Its body is monstrous, its face resembles an oni, and fire burns between the gaps in its armored plates.

This is Supaida.

Supaida possesses extraordinary agility, pyro-like powers, and webs strong enough to rival steel. It uses the cavern itself as a weapon, creating traps, sealing routes, and forcing its prey into increasingly dangerous positions.
The creature has made the cave its hunting ground. Its webs cover old passages and abandoned equipment, making it difficult to distinguish natural formations from traps.

[Boss] - Supaida

The fight combines movement, environmental hazards, and opportunities to exploit the spider's own webbing.

Supaida attempts to immobilize the hero before attacking with fire. The hero must avoid becoming trapped while finding opportunities to damage its armored body.

As the fight progresses, the heat in the cavern becomes increasingly dangerous. Webs ignite, platforms collapse, and the hero must keep moving to avoid being cornered.

When Supaida is defeated, its webbing begins to burn away, revealing a path deeper into the mountain.

The hero can return toward the Scouts' Camp or continue the ascent.

Beyond the cavern, the terrain becomes warmer, and ash begins falling from the sky.

The Red-Maned Wolf's territory is near.
