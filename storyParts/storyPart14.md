[Part 14] - The Forthcoming

[The Arrival]

The golden light spreads across the entire kingdom.

The ground fractures, mountains tremble, and the ruins of Haldritch begin to change. The False Dawn has reached its final stage.

Far beyond the palace, something enormous begins to emerge.

The Forthcoming is an ancient entity whose physical form resembles a colossal tree-like abomination. Its body is formed from twisted black roots and branches, its limbs stretch across the landscape, and golden eyes shine from within its tangled head.

As it rises, the sky becomes brighter.

The event and the entity are revealed to be two parts of the same catastrophe.

The False Dawn is the process through which the Forthcoming enters the world. The entity itself is the source of the devastation.

The Oracle's prophecy was never simply a warning that the world would end.

It was a warning that something would arrive to end it.

[The Worldroot]

The hero returns to the ruins of Haldritch as the Forthcoming begins spreading its roots across the landscape.

The entity's presence distorts reality. Buildings become twisted reflections of their former selves, paths shift unexpectedly, and the sky displays fragments of possible futures.

The hero must make their way toward the creature's central body while surviving the destruction caused by its enormous limbs.

[Boss phase one] — The Awakening

The Forthcoming attacks with its roots, sweeping them across the battlefield and tearing apart sections of the ruined city.

The hero must avoid the creature's attacks while destroying the roots that anchor it to the ground.

[Boss phase two] — The Golden Catastrophe

The False Dawn intensifies.

Golden energy spreads across the arena, creating dangerous areas where reality becomes unstable. The Forthcoming summons enormous branches from the earth and attempts to trap the hero beneath its body.

The hero must use the environment and their accumulated knowledge to reach the entity's vulnerable points.

[Boss phase three] — The Exposed Core

As its roots are destroyed, the Forthcoming's central body becomes vulnerable.

A glowing core appears within its tangled mass of black branches. The hero must strike the core while avoiding the creature's most powerful attacks.

Dawnbreaker reacts to the core, revealing its true purpose.

The weapon can sever the connection between the Forthcoming and the world.

But doing so requires the hero to get close enough to strike the entity directly.
