[Part 13] - Alternate X’s True Purpose

[The Hunter Returns]

As the hero leaves the sanctuary, a familiar sound echoes across the mountain.

Alternate X has survived.

Its damaged body has continued to regenerate, and its exoskeleton bears the scars of the hero's previous encounters. Yet its behavior has changed. It no longer seems focused solely on hunting the hero.

It is looking toward the golden sky.

The hero realizes that Alternate X has been following the same supernatural signals that led them to the sanctuary.

The creature was designed to fight the Forthcoming, and it recognizes the entity's growing influence.

Alternate X attacks, driven by an instinct that its creators never fully understood.

[Boss] - Alternate X

This time, the hero has Dawnbreaker and a deeper understanding of the creature's weaknesses.

The fight progresses through three stages:

Adaptation: Alternate X uses its physical strength and reinforced exoskeleton to overwhelm the hero.

Instability: The damage it has sustained begins to affect its movements, exposing weaknesses in its armor.

The Signal: The False Dawn intensifies, causing Alternate X to react violently to the sky. The hero exploits the opening and finally brings the supersoldier down.

Alternate X collapses.

The king's greatest creation has finally been defeated.

But the sky continues to glow.

The creature was never the source of the apocalypse. It was a weapon created to oppose it.

The Forthcoming is still coming.
