[Part 15] - The End of the False Dawn

[The Deliverer's Choice]

The hero reaches the Forthcoming's exposed core.

The world is collapsing around them. The golden sky burns above Haldritch, and the entity's roots extend toward the mountain and the distant forest.

The hero realizes that defeating the Forthcoming will not simply mean killing a monster.

They must use Dawnbreaker to sever its connection to the world, ending the False Dawn before the entity can fully establish itself.

The final strike is the culmination of everything the hero has learned.

The Lantern of the Lost revealed hidden paths. The Oracle's knowledge explained the prophecy. The Gaping Serpent's cavern exposed the ancient energy beneath the mountain. The king's records revealed the purpose of Alternate X. Dawnbreaker provides the means to confront the threat itself.

The hero raises the weapon and strikes.

The golden light begins to fade.

The Forthcoming releases a sound that shakes the ruins, and its enormous body begins to collapse. The roots withdraw from the earth, the sky fractures, and the supernatural radiance disappears.

For a moment, the world falls silent.

The False Dawn has ended.

But what remains of Haldritch will depend on the hero's choices throughout the story.

[A Future Reclaimed]

If the hero succeeds, the survivors emerge from their shelters and witness the first natural sunrise in generations.
Clara begins organizing the rebuilding of the kingdom, not as a return to the old monarchy, but as an opportunity to create something better.

The Oracle's visions finally become quiet.

The mountain stops trembling, and the unnatural energy that surrounded the Gaping Serpent's cavern begins to fade. The forest remains restored, and the Scouts' Camp becomes the foundation of a new settlement.

Alternate X's fate remains a painful reminder of the king's ambition. It was created to save humanity, but the king's obsession with controlling the future led to the destruction of his kingdom.

The hero's victory does not erase that tragedy.

It ensures that the tragedy does not become the end of humanity.
