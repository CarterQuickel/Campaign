[Part 12] - The Final Ascent

[Beyond the Serpent's Domain]

With the Gaping Serpent defeated, the hero can travel farther into the mountain.

The passage beyond its cavern leads to a forgotten sanctuary built by a civilization that predates Haldritch. Its walls bear symbols resembling those recorded by the Oracle, and its architecture suggests that its builders understood something about the Forthcoming long before the king began his experiments.

The hero discovers that the mountain was never merely a place where dangerous creatures lived.

It was a place where the influence of the Forthcoming could be observed.

The Gaping Serpent had inhabited this region for centuries. The strange energy beneath the mountain may have sustained the creature, affected its behavior, or been something it instinctively guarded.

The hero cannot determine which explanation is correct.

But the sanctuary's inscriptions reveal that the False Dawn has occurred before, and that the Forthcoming has attempted to enter the world in earlier ages.

The ancient civilization survived long enough to record its warnings.

It did not leave a clear explanation of how the entity was defeated.

Instead, the inscriptions refer repeatedly to a weapon carried by a figure known as the Deliverer.

That weapon was Dawnbreaker.

[Dawnbreaker]

The hero reaches a sealed chamber deep within the sanctuary.

The weapon rests behind an ancient mechanism designed to respond to the presence of someone willing to confront the Forthcoming without knowing the outcome.

The hero is confronted with visions of possible futures.

In one, Haldritch is restored and humanity survives under a new ruler.

In another, Alternate X stands victorious over a ruined world.

In a third, the hero defeats the Forthcoming but loses everything they sought to protect.

The visions offer no guarantee that any one future will occur.

The hero must proceed despite that uncertainty.

When the trial is complete, the seal opens.

Dawnbreaker is revealed: a legendary weapon created to shatter the False Dawn and sever the Forthcoming's connection to the world.

The hero takes the weapon.

For the first time, they possess something designed specifically for the threat that has haunted Haldritch since the beginning of the story.

But the False Dawn is already spreading.

There is little time left.
