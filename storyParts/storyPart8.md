[Part 8] - The Red-Maned Wolf

[The burning pass]

The hero emerges from the caves into a region of black rock, burning vegetation, and constant ashfall.

The landscape has been scorched by repeated eruptions of magical fire. The few trees that remain are charred and twisted, and the wind carries the smell of smoke from distant ridges.

At the highest point of the pass lies the territory of the Red-Maned Wolf.

Unlike Supaida, which relies on traps and ambushes, the wolf is a direct and overwhelming opponent. Its body is powerful enough to break stone, and its fiery magic can turn the battlefield into an inferno.

The hero discovers signs that the wolf has been fighting something else. Deep gouges cross the rocks, and several enormous trees have been torn from the ground.

The wolf is not simply guarding a path. It is defending its territory against a presence that has begun to emerge farther up the mountain.

[The beast of fire]

The Red-Maned Wolf appears on a ridge above the hero.

Its mane burns with an unnatural red flame. Its eyes glow through the smoke, and its growl causes loose stones to tremble beneath the hero's feet.

The wolf attacks without warning.

[Boss] - Red-Maned Wolf

The fight revolves around strength, fire, and timing.

The wolf charges across the arena, uses powerful bites and claw strikes, and releases waves of fire that force the hero away from safe positions.

As it becomes injured, its attacks grow more destructive. The flames spread across the battlefield, and the hero must avoid being caught between the wolf and the surrounding hazards.
When defeated, the wolf collapses.

For a brief moment, the mountain becomes quiet.
Then the hero hears it.

A deafening hiss rolls down from somewhere above the pass. It is louder than anything the hero has heard before, and it seems to reverberate through the mountain itself.

The Red-Maned Wolf had been guarding the lower approach, but whatever lives above it is far more enormous.
The hero looks toward the upper mountain.

Something moves behind the ash clouds.

The hero decides to return to the Scouts' Camp and rest before continuing.

The following morning, the scouts have disturbing news.

An expedition that had been sent to map the upper mountain has disappeared.

Their last report mentioned a cavern, a series of abandoned royal research stations, and something moving beneath the stone.

One of the surviving scouts describes the creature's trail.

It was not a series of footprints.

It was a trench.

The name appears in an old expedition record, written by a royal surveyor who never returned:

The Gaping Serpent.
