[Part 2] - The King Who Lost Everything

The hero enters the Fallen Palace through a collapsed outer wall. The halls are filled with broken statues, abandoned royal weapons, and remnants of the king's personal guard.

The palace tells the story of its own destruction.

Orders are still nailed to walls. Research notes are scattered across the floors. Some rooms were destroyed by fire, while others appear to have been torn apart from the inside.

The deeper the hero travels, the more evidence they find of the king's deteriorating judgment. His early documents speak of protecting the people. His later writings describe human lives as acceptable losses in the pursuit of salvation.

At the heart of the palace lies the former throne room.

The Fallen King awaits the hero, wielding King's Ruin, the sword he carried during the final days of Haldritch.

The king no longer resembles the ruler depicted in the palace's statues. His armor is damaged, his body is worn by years of fighting, and his mind has been consumed by the belief that everything he did was necessary.

He sees the hero as another obstacle between himself and salvation.

[Boss] - The Fallen King

The fight begins with the king attacking like a trained warrior, using disciplined sword strikes and defensive maneuvers. As the battle progresses, his composure breaks. He becomes more aggressive, abandoning defense in favor of powerful, desperate attacks.

When defeated, the king collapses beside his throne.

He does not beg for his life. Instead, he asks the hero whether they truly believe the world can survive what is coming.

Before he can explain further, the palace begins to shake.

Something has arrived.

[First Alternate X Encounter]

A heavy impact echoes from the palace entrance.

Then another.

The hero hears metal bending, stone breaking, and something breathing in the darkness beyond the throne room.

Alternate X emerges.

Its enormous muscular frame is covered by a reinforced exoskeleton, its movements unnaturally fast for something so large. It does not behave like an ordinary beast. It studies the hero, adapts to their movements, and attacks with frightening precision.

The hero's weapons barely penetrate its outer shell.

Alternate X is not a boss the hero is expected to kill at this point. It is a survival encounter.

The hero must discover that certain areas of its exoskeleton are vulnerable. By striking these weak points, the hero can destabilize its movements and temporarily stun it.

The opening allows the hero to escape the palace.

As the hero flees, Alternate X recovers and releases a furious roar. It does not pursue immediately, instead watching the hero disappear into the ruins.

For a moment, it seems almost confused.

The hero has survived something that should have been impossible.

But Alternate X has now recognized them.

And it will hunt them throughout the journey.
