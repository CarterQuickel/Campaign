[Part 5] - The Crown Beneath the Rubble

[The King’s Hidden Chambers]

With the Lantern of the Lost recovered, Clara asks the hero to return to the palace with her.

This is the second part of her quest.

The lantern reveals hidden markings and concealed passages that were invisible during the first visit. Together, the hero and Clara explore sections of the palace that were previously inaccessible.

The light leads them back to the throne room where the hero fought the Fallen King.

As the lantern illuminates the rubble, a faint glimmer appears beneath a collapsed section of stone.

The hero clears the debris and discovers a crown.

Clara freezes.

Her expression changes from curiosity to recognition. She approaches the hero and carefully lifts the crown from their hands.
Tears stream down her face.

She whispers:

"Oh, how I've missed you, Father..."

For the first time, Clara's composure breaks completely.

She explains that the crown belonged to her father, the king of Haldritch. Before the kingdom fell, she was his daughter and heir. She survived the collapse but disappeared from royal life, choosing to remain among the people who were trying to rebuild the kingdom.

She had never been able to return to the throne room and confront what remained of her father's reign.

The Lantern of the Lost has finally allowed her to see the place clearly again.

After a moment, Clara hands the crown to the hero.

"Thank you for letting me see him one last time. Take the crown. It acts as a key to my father's hidden chambers, for when you're prepared."
The hero now possesses the Crown of Haldritch.

The crown is not merely a royal symbol. It opens the king's hidden chambers, where records of his research and the final days of Haldritch may still remain.

However, Clara asks the hero to wait before entering. She needs time to accept what happened to her father.
The hero returns to camp with her.

[The Road Divides]

With Clara's quest complete, the hero reaches a turning point.

Two paths are available:

- The Oracle's Domain: Return to the Oracle to learn more about the Forthcoming and the hero's purpose.

- The Mountain: Pursue the next major stage of the journey through the dangerous mountain passes, where three formidable bosses await.

The paths can be completed in either order, allowing the player to choose between seeking knowledge and facing immediate danger.

The crown also provides a future route back into the palace's hidden chambers, so the story's mysteries need not be resolved all at once.
