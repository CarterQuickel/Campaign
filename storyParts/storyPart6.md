[Part 6] -The Oracle’s Choice

If the hero chooses to revisit the Oracle, they must travel beyond the ruins of Haldritch to the Oracle's Domain.

The journey passes through an area where the land seems untouched by the kingdom's destruction. Ancient stones hover above the ground, distant lights move against the wind, and the sky occasionally reveals stars that should not be visible during the day.

At the end of the route stands a tall magical tower.

The Oracle's Domain has existed for generations, serving as a place where visions of the future are studied and recorded. The tower's interior is filled with records of past prophecies, many of which have been deliberately sealed away.

The Oracle welcomes the hero and acknowledges their progress.

They know about the Fallen King, Alternate X, and the Lantern of the Lost. They also know that the hero has begun uncovering the truth behind Haldritch's collapse.

But the Oracle does not offer a complete explanation.

Instead, they reveal that the Forthcoming is not a single event that can be stopped by defeating one enemy. It is a future toward which the world is being drawn. The Oracle has seen many possible outcomes, but every vision ends in uncertainty.

The king believed that Alternate X would guarantee humanity's survival.

The Oracle never promised that.

The Oracle's original prophecy warned that the Forthcoming would come, but the king interpreted the warning as a problem that could be solved through force.

The hero must now decide whether the Oracle is an ally or another powerful figure whose intentions cannot be trusted.

Fight the Oracle or stand beside them.

The Oracle presents the hero with a choice.

[Path A] - Challenge the Oracle
The hero believes the Oracle has withheld too much information, or decides that the only way to understand their power is to confront it directly.
The Oracle accepts the challenge.

The battle takes place in a chamber where the environment shifts according to the Oracle's visions. Attacks appear before they are launched, platforms disappear into possible futures, and the hero must respond to illusions of events that have not yet happened.

The Oracle fights with precision rather than brute strength. They anticipate the hero's movements and use their understanding of possible futures to create difficult encounters.

When defeated, the Oracle acknowledges the hero's strength and grants them the Oracle Eye.

The Oracle Eye allows the hero to perceive fragments of possible futures. It can reveal hidden paths, expose deceptive visions, and provide glimpses of danger before it occurs.

However, it does not show a single guaranteed future. Every vision is incomplete, and the hero must decide how to interpret what they see.
The Oracle survives the encounter, but the relationship between them and the hero is changed.

[Path B] - Stand with the Oracle

The hero chooses to trust the Oracle and hear what they have to say.

The Oracle reveals that their visions have become increasingly unstable. The Forthcoming appears in different forms, and the same events sometimes lead to contradictory outcomes.

The Oracle believes that someone must investigate the source of this instability.

They cannot leave the tower for long without losing control over the visions they maintain. Instead, they give the hero the Prophecy Scroll, a collection of recorded prophecies that also serves as a means of communicating with the Oracle across great distances.

The Oracle promises to help the hero interpret future discoveries.

The hero leaves with a potential ally, although the Oracle's incomplete knowledge remains a source of uncertainty.

[Consequences of the choice]

Both paths provide a useful reward and a way to interact with the Oracle's knowledge throughout the campaign.
The distinction is primarily narrative:

The Oracle Eye lets the hero discover information through personal visions.

The Prophecy Scroll lets the hero consult the Oracle and receive guidance remotely.

Neither choice should immediately reveal the entire truth about the Forthcoming. The most important revelations are still waiting in the mountain and the king's hidden chambers.
