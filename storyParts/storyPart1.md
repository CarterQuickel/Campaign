[Part 1] - Prologue

Long before the kingdom of Haldritch fell, the Oracle delivered a prophecy that would eventually shape the fate of humanity.

The prophecy spoke of an approaching event known only as the Forthcoming—a catastrophe said to bring about the end of humanity. No one knew exactly when it would occur, what form it would take, or whether it could be prevented. The Oracle could see fragments of what was to come, but never the complete picture.

The king of Haldritch became obsessed with preventing this future. At first, his efforts were praised. He poured royal resources into research, recruited the kingdom's finest scholars, and ordered expeditions into ancient ruins in search of a way to defy the prophecy.

Eventually, the royal dungeons became a research facility. Its researchers began experimenting with unnatural biological enhancements, hoping to create a warrior capable of surviving anything the Forthcoming might unleash.

Their greatest achievement was Alternate X.

Alternate X was a supersoldier unlike anything humanity had seen. Its body possessed extraordinary strength, durability, and regenerative capabilities. Its muscular frame was reinforced by an exoskeleton that could withstand attacks that would destroy ordinary soldiers. Even its senses surpassed the limits of human biology.

The king believed he had finally found his answer to the Oracle's prophecy.

But Alternate X was never truly understood.

One day, it escaped containment.

The escape devastated the royal dungeons. Researchers vanished, entire sections of the palace were destroyed, and the king's soldiers proved incapable of subduing their creation. Whatever the researchers had built, they had created something they could no longer control.
The king's fear became madness.

He redirected the kingdom's remaining resources toward stopping Alternate X and preventing the Forthcoming. He imposed harsher laws, seized supplies, abandoned settlements, and sacrificed his own people to fund increasingly desperate experiments.
His kingdom slowly collapsed under the weight of his obsession.

The people began calling him the Fallen King long before he was defeated.

Haldritch shattered. Its royal roads fell into ruin, its villages emptied, and its once-proud palace became a graveyard of stone and ash.

Yet Alternate X remained alive.

And somewhere beyond the ruins, the Forthcoming continued to approach.

Years later, a hero arrives in Haldritch.

The hero has been summoned by the Oracle, who believes they may be the only person capable of confronting the Fallen King, stopping Alternate X, and facing the Forthcoming.
The hero possesses a strange primal state in which they cannot be killed. This makes them uniquely suited to survive the horrors of Haldritch, but it does not make every encounter easy. Enemies can overwhelm, restrain, incapacitate, or force the hero to retreat.

The Oracle does not explain everything immediately. Their visions are incomplete, and they seem to know more about the hero's purpose than they are willing to reveal.

The hero's journey begins at the entrance to the ruined kingdom.

In the distance, the remains of the palace tower over the landscape. Beyond it lies the Scouts' Camp, a settlement established by survivors and scavengers trying to reclaim what remains of their home.

But before the hero can reach the camp, they must enter the place where the king's downfall became irreversible.

The Fallen Palace.
