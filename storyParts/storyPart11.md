[Part 11] - The Forthcoming Approaches

[The Signs of the False Dawn] 

When the hero returns to the Scouts' Camp, the survivors have begun reporting strange changes in the world.
The sky occasionally turns gold, even in the middle of the night. Animals flee the surrounding wilderness, and scouts have discovered patches of land where trees and rocks have changed shape overnight.

The Oracle recognizes these signs.

The False Dawn has begun.

The False Dawn is the apocalyptic event foretold in the prophecy: the period during which the Forthcoming's influence spreads into the world and prepares the way for its arrival.

The Forthcoming itself is the entity responsible for the event.

The Oracle explains that the two are inseparable. As the entity approaches, reality begins to change. The golden sky is not sunlight, but a manifestation of the force pressing against the boundaries of the world.

The hero has not yet encountered the Forthcoming directly, but its presence is becoming impossible to ignore.

[The Weapon of the Deliverer]

Among the king's records, the hero discovers references to an ancient weapon called Dawnbreaker.

The weapon was created to oppose the False Dawn and defy the Forthcoming. Its purpose was not simply to destroy powerful enemies, but to sever the connection between the entity and the world.

The king knew of Dawnbreaker, but his research never established its location.

Other records suggest that the weapon was hidden in an ancient sanctuary beyond the mountain, where the first known accounts of the Forthcoming were recorded.

The hero now has a new objective.

The king attempted to create a weapon strong enough to kill the Forthcoming. The hero must find the weapon that was specifically made to confront it.

The distinction may be the difference between defeating the entity and merely delaying the apocalypse.
