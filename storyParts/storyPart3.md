[Part 3] - The Scouts’ Camp

After escaping Alternate X, the hero wanders through the ruins of Haldritch.

The streets are no longer recognizable as the roads of a prosperous kingdom. Buildings lean against one another, old market stalls lie buried beneath rubble, and strange creatures have made their homes among the abandoned structures.

Eventually, the hero discovers a settlement known as the Scouts' Camp.

The camp is a permanent refuge for scouts, surveyors, scavengers, and survivors who have dedicated themselves to recovering what remains of Haldritch. Its inhabitants map the ruins, retrieve supplies, and search for information about the king's experiments.

Although the camp offers relative safety, its people are far from comfortable. Supplies are scarce, expeditions frequently disappear, and the threat of Alternate X prevents anyone from believing the kingdom can ever truly recover.

The hero is approached by Clara, a young woman dressed in regal clothing that seems strangely out of place among the survivors.

Clara is polite but guarded. Unlike the other scouts, she speaks of the kingdom as something personal rather than a place she hopes to reclaim.

She has a request.

[Clara’s Quest] - Lantern of the Lost

Clara asks the hero to retrieve an artifact from the Forsaken Forest: the Lantern of the Lost.

The forest was once a peaceful region of Haldritch, but after the kingdom's collapse, it became shrouded in unnatural darkness. Travelers who enter frequently lose their way. Some return unable to distinguish reality from hallucination; others never return at all.

Clara believes the Lantern of the Lost can restore the forest and reveal hidden paths through places obscured by the corruption.
She cannot explain exactly how she knows this. She only insists that the lantern is important.

Before departing, the hero may explore the camp and speak to its inhabitants.

[Optional quest] — Scout Corvus

Scout Corvus needs help retrieving specific supplies from the ruins. These materials are necessary to construct a Ruinclimber, a grappling hook designed to help scouts traverse collapsed buildings and broken terrain.

Completing the quest rewards the hero with the Ruinclimber.

This optional quest has a later consequence: when Alternate X returns, the grappling hook provides a safer and faster escape route. If the hero skips the quest, the second encounter with Alternate X becomes another dangerous stun-and-escape sequence.

The quest remains available after the hero returns from the forest.

[The reforger]

At the camp, the hero can also meet a reforger who recognizes King's Ruin.

He explains that the sword was once a much finer weapon. Its present condition is the result of years of damage, not simply age. He believes he can restore it, but needs a rare crystal known as Fractured Fate.

The reforger cannot say where the crystal can be found. He has heard rumors of objects like it appearing in places touched by strange energies.
For now, the hero must continue the main quest.
