[Part 4] - The Forsaken Forest

[Follow the Lights]

Once the hero enters the Forsaken Forest, the route back to Haldritch becomes inaccessible. Dense growth, unstable paths, and the forest's unnatural influence prevent a safe retreat.

Glowing wisps begin appearing between the trees.

They drift ahead of the hero, illuminating a narrow path through the darkness. Their light is the only reliable guide.

Soon, a cloaked man carrying a lantern approaches.

He warns the hero:

"These wisps will guide you through this dark forest. Do not stray from them, for those who remain in the darkness too long have all lost their minds."

The cloaked man knows the forest well, but refuses to explain who he is or how he survived there.

The hero must follow the wisps through a series of environmental challenges. Broken platforms, deep ravines, hostile creatures, and misleading lights force the hero to pay attention to the route.

The darkness is not merely an absence of light. It distorts perception, creating false paths and making familiar landmarks appear unfamiliar.

As the hero progresses, they discover abandoned camps belonging to people who attempted to reach the forest's heart. Some left warnings scratched into trees. Others left journals describing voices that called to them from beyond the wisps.

The forest appears to be protecting something.

[Death Blossom]

Eventually, the hero finds the cloaked man standing before a massive opening in the forest.

Beyond it lies the heart of the Forsaken Forest.

The cloaked man explains that the beast known as Death Blossom guards the Lantern of the Lost. It has become bound to the forest's corruption, and the lantern cannot be retrieved while the creature remains alive.

He offers no alternative.

The hero enters the clearing.

Death Blossom is a monstrous plant-like creature with enormous petals, thorned limbs, and roots that spread through the ground. It attacks by creating barriers of vines, launching poisonous growths, and using the terrain to trap its opponent.

Its roots have spread throughout the forest, feeding on whatever strange energy has taken hold there.

[Boss]- Death Blossom

During the fight, the hero must destroy the roots feeding the creature before attacking its central body. As the battle progresses, the clearing becomes increasingly unstable, forcing the hero to move between safe areas.

When Death Blossom falls, the corruption begins to recede.

The glowing wisps grow brighter, the oppressive darkness lifts, and the trees begin to recover their natural colors.
At the heart of the clearing, the hero finds the Lantern of the Lost.

The cloaked man watches the forest return to life. For the first time, he appears relieved.

He tells the hero that the forest will remember what was done here.

When the hero turns to ask what he means, the man has disappeared.

[Second Alternate X Encounter]

The hero returns to the ruins with the Lantern of the Lost.

But the journey back is interrupted.

Alternate X appears among the shattered buildings, blocking the hero's route. Its exoskeleton bears scars from their previous encounter, but the damage has not stopped it.

This time, the hero has a potential advantage.

If they completed Corvus's quest, the Ruinclimber allows them to reach a collapsed structure and escape across the ruins before Alternate X can catch them.

If they do not possess the Ruinclimber, they must repeat the earlier encounter's central challenge: find vulnerable points in Alternate X's exoskeleton, stun it, and escape before it recovers.

The encounter reinforces an important truth about Alternate X: the hero is learning how to survive it, but is nowhere near ready to defeat it.
The hero eventually reaches the Scouts' Camp.

Clara is waiting.
