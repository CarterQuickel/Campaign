Here are a list of some enemies, bosses, and NPCs that can be incorporated into the story.

Story 1:
========================================================================
1. Withstander
    - This monstrosity wields a large gong, which is used to contain Alternate X in case of his escape. He rings it to stun those that try and step out of line. His attacks in the campaign would reflect that.
    - TYPE: ENEMY
2. Alternate V
    - An earlier itartion of ALternate X that failed the tests provided. Disregarded when its successor escaped, its body finally revived with a fit of fury and yearning for revenge.
    TYPE: BOSS
3. Daughter's Consort
    - A child who once went everywhere with the king. Now, she's alone and scared, for her life has been taken from her as the Forthcoming is inevitable. Complete her quest and see who her consort is.
    TYPE: NPC
